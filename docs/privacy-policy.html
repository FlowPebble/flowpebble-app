100% Offline-First Core Guarantee: FlowPebble does not require user registration, accounts, or cloud logins. All micro-guides you create, bookmark, check off, or complete are stored exclusively on your local Android device inside an offline SQLite (Room) database and Jetpack DataStore preferences.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

FlowPebble stores starter micro-guides, custom user-authored micro-guides (titles, summaries, categories, and 3-to-5 step checklists), step completion bitmasks, bookmark states, theme preferences (Light/Dark/System), and haptic feedback settings locally on your device using Android Room SQLite database and Jetpack Preferences DataStore.

We do not operate a proprietary remote server that collects, syncs, or reads your personal micro-guides. If you clear the application's storage or uninstall FlowPebble, locally stored custom guides and progress are permanently deleted from your device (subject to your own Android system backup settings).

2. Advertising & Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK

To support free development and maintenance, FlowPebble integrates the official Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) (App ID: ca-app-pub-8550257135927026~2543487910) to display clearly separated Anchored Adaptive Banner ads, frequency-capped Interstitial ads at natural completion boundaries, and strictly voluntary Rewarded video ads (used to unlock the Pro Guide Vault).

When advertisements are requested and consented to, Google and its certified advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain device and interaction signals, including:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID), which you can reset or delete at any time in your Android Settings (“Settings → Google → Ads”).
  • Coarse Location & Network Information: IP address (used to infer approximate country/region for regulatory compliance and relevant ad delivery) and network connection type.
  • Ad Interaction & Diagnostics Data: Ad views, voluntary clicks, rewarded video completions, app launch performance, and crash diagnostics related to the ad SDK.

For full details on how Google processes data in partner apps, please review How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. User Messaging Platform (UMP) & GDPR / CCPA / US State Privacy Rights

FlowPebble integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to respect your regional privacy rights under the European Economic Area (EEA) General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and applicable US state privacy laws.

  • Consent Before Ad Initialization: FlowPebble verifies your consent status via the UMP SDK before initializing the AdMob SDK or requesting ads.
  • Revoking or Updating Consent Anytime: Users in applicable regions can open Settings & Privacy → Advertising Privacy Choices inside FlowPebble at any time to review, modify, or withdraw advertising consent choices.

4. User-Initiated Sharing

When you tap “Share this guide” or “Share FlowPebble”, the app formats your selected guide text and invokes Android's native system Share Sheet (Intent.ACTION_SEND). FlowPebble never transmits your guide to third-party social networks automatically; you control which messaging, email, or note-taking app receives the text.

5. Permissions Requested

FlowPebble follows strict least-privilege security principles and requests only four technical permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET & android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely to detect online/offline status, display UMP consent forms, and load AdMob advertisements when online.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used for subtle tactile haptic feedback when checking off steps (can be toggled off in Settings).
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK subject to your device settings and UMP consent.

FlowPebble never requests access to your Camera, Microphone, Fine/Coarse GPS Location, Contacts, Phone State, or Photos/Media library.

6. Children's Privacy & Target Audience

FlowPebble is designed for a general audience aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us via email support, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

7. Data Security & Retention

FlowPebble enforces HTTPS-only network communication (cleartextTrafficPermitted="false"). Local guide data remains on your device only for as long as you keep the app installed.
